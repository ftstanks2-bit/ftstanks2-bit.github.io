Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FTS Tanks ("FTS", "we", "us") handles information when authorised employees and other approved users access FTS StockMaster (the "Service").

1. Information the Service handles

Depending on how the Service is used, it may store or process:

  • Account information used to sign in, such as email address and user or role details.
  • Stock and inventory information, including item descriptions, quantities, costs, receipts, adjustments and stock movements.
  • Operational information entered by users, such as purchase orders, supplier details, job references, bills of materials, allocations, picking slips, recipients and audit reasons.
  • Email details needed to send a user-requested report, including the recipient address and the report attachment.

Please do not enter personal information into the Service unless it is necessary for an authorised business activity.

2. How information is used

FTS uses this information to operate and maintain stock control and purchasing workflows, manage user access, keep operational and audit records, prepare reports, send emails requested by authorised users, and investigate faults or protect the Service.

3. Service providers and email

The Service uses Supabase for application authentication and database services. When an authorised user requests an email from the Service, it uses Google Gmail services to send that message from the configured FTS sender account. Information necessary to provide these functions is processed by those providers under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

The Gmail connection is used for sending Service-generated emails. The Service does not need access to read the sender's general Gmail inbox for this email-sending function.

4. Access and disclosure

Access to business information is intended for users authorised by FTS and is subject to the access controls configured for the Service. FTS does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described above, or disclosed where required by law, to protect the Service, or to address a legitimate security or operational issue.

5. Security

FTS uses account authentication, configured user permissions and service-provider security features to help protect information. No online service or method of storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep their sign-in details private and report suspected unauthorised access to FTS.

6. Retention and deletion

Business records may be retained for as long as needed for operational, audit, legal or record-keeping purposes. Retention and deletion may also depend on FTS business requirements and the capabilities of the underlying service providers. Contact FTS to request access, correction or deletion where appropriate; some business records may need to be retained.

7. Your choices and requests

For questions about this policy, or requests relating to information in FTS StockMaster, contact FTS using the details below. Please include enough information for us to identify the relevant account or record, but do not send passwords, OAuth tokens or other secret credentials by email.

8. Changes to this policy

FTS may update this policy when the Service or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.
